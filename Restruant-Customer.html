Platform Features

A seamless experience for
both sides of the counter.

🍽️
Smart Menu Navigation
Customers browse full menus in chat — with photos, prices, allergen tags, and dietary filters. Updated in real time from the restaurant dashboard.
🛒
Customisable Cart
Granular modifier support — extra cheese, no onions, spicy level 2. Persistent multi-item cart that remembers every preference.
📍
Live Order Tracking
Customers get proactive push updates at every stage — Confirmed, In Kitchen, Out for Delivery, Arrived. No more "where's my food?" calls.
📅
Table Reservations
Interactive booking with live availability checks. Digital waitlist queue with instant text alerts when a table opens up.
💳
In-Chat Payments
Stripe and Razorpay built in. Customers pay by card, UPI, or wallet without ever leaving the conversation.
🤝
Human Handoff
When something needs a real person, the bot steps aside instantly — with full chat context handed to the team. No repeating. No friction.
